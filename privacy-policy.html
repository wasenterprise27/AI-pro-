1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to AI pro 001 (also distributed as Infinity Content AI), an advanced creative suite engineered to help digital creators, marketers, and businesses produce viral social media captions, video scripts, affiliate copy, and campaign schedules.

We hold your privacy and intellectual property in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is handled, the role of server-side Artificial Intelligence integrations, and the security measures safeguarding your creative work.

Core Guarantee: You retain 100% intellectual property and commercial rights to all text, scripts, prompts, and content created using this application. We never claim ownership of your generated creations.

2. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization and collect only the information necessary to fulfill application features:

  • Account Credentials & Authentication: When you sign in via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic public profile details (display name, email address, and avatar URL) strictly for identifying your account and securing saved presets.
  • User-Authored Content & Inputs: Content topics, campaign titles, product URLs (such as Amazon affiliate links), brand kit guidelines, target audience niches, and generation preferences entered into the generator tools.
  • Local Workspace Data: Saved draft notes, favorited scripts, schedule entries on your content calendar, and locally stored templates are kept on your device storage via an encrypted on-device database (Android Room).
  • Device & Diagnostic Telemetry: Standard non-identifying technical metadata including OS version, app version code (3, v1.2.0), network state, and crash logs to maintain stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for the following operational purposes:

  1. Generative Content Production: Synthesizing inputs into scene-by-scene video scripts, platform-specific hooks, hashtags, and marketing copy.
  2. Personalization & Brand Kits: Storing your custom brand tone, colors, and typography preferences across creation tools.
  3. Workspace Productivity: Synchronizing production due dates with Google Tasks and attaching asset briefs from Google Drive.
  4. Subscription & Quota Management: Enforcing tier limits (Free tier daily generations vs. Pro unlimited processing).
  5. Application Health: Diagnosing errors, resolving ANR (Application Not Responding) events, and optimizing load speeds.

4. AI Processing & LLM Data Protection

Our application communicates with Google Gemini API models (including Gemini 3.5 Flash) to generate content.

No Model Training: Under our enterprise API architecture, your prompts, product URLs, video outlines, and outputs are never used to train, retrain, or improve foundational AI models. Your creative ideas remain confidential and exclusive to you.

Data sent to the Gemini API is processed transiently over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels and is not permanently retained by the LLM providers for commercial exploitation.

5. Google Workspace API User Data Policy

When you enable optional Google Workspace features within the app, the application requests access to the following Google APIs:

Service OAuth Scope Purpose & Data Handling
Google Tasks tasks Allows creators to sync content schedules to Google Tasks and import task titles into prompt drafts. We do not inspect personal non-app tasks.
Google Contacts contacts.readonly Allows creators to select sponsor or agency contacts to share generated script packages with one click. Contacts are never uploaded to third parties.
Google Drive Picker drive.file Enables selecting brand guides or media files from Drive for prompt context. Only user-selected files are accessed.
Google Limited Use Compliance: The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

For users on our Free tier, the application may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and interaction data to serve ads.

You can adjust your ad personalization preferences at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.

7. Device Permissions & Purpose

The Android application requests the following permissions only when required by feature actions:

  • CAMERA: Requested strictly when you choose to capture live reference photos for thumbnail styling or generative image prompts. Camera access is never initiated in the background.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to contact the Gemini API, sync Google Workspace endpoints, and load real-time analytics.
  • VIBRATE: Used for subtle haptic feedback during interactive prompt generation and copy events.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional on Android 13+ to notify you when scheduled social releases or video renders complete.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain user account data only as long as your account remains active. Because creative content is stored in your device's local Room database, you maintain direct control over your records.

Right to Deletion: You can delete all your stored data, local drafts, and account history at any time.

How to delete your data:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Creator Profile > Delete Account. This immediately purges all local databases, cached media, and sign-in tokens.
  • Web / Remote Request: Submit an account deletion request directly to our privacy officer by email at stephenampangwapyalenge@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - AI pro 001". All associated cloud records will be permanently eradicated within 48 business hours.

9. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to secure your personal data:

  • End-to-end cryptographic transit security using HTTPS and TLS 1.3.
  • Zero storage of raw user passwords (delegated entirely to secure OAuth2 and Google Identity tokens).
  • Sandboxed Android internal application storage preventing other apps from accessing your local drafts.

10. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Obtain an export of your saved scripts, presets, and history in standard text format.
  • Right of Rectification: Update or modify your profile name, email, or niche preferences via the in-app Settings dialog.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request total deletion of your profile and data records.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third parties.

11. Children's Privacy

AI pro 001 is intended for general audiences and content creators aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us immediately so we can promptly delete the data.
