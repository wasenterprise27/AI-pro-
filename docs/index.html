1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to AI pro 001 (also distributed as Infinity Content AI), an advanced creative suite engineered to help digital creators, marketers, and businesses produce viral social media captions, video scripts, affiliate copy, and campaign schedules.

Core Guarantee: You retain 100% intellectual property and commercial rights to all text, scripts, prompts, and content created using this application. We never claim ownership of your generated creations.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials & Authentication: Basic public profile details (display name, email address, avatar URL) via Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth strictly for securing saved presets.
  • User-Authored Content & Inputs: Content topics, campaign titles, product URLs, brand kit guidelines, target audience niches, and generation preferences.
  • Local Workspace Data: Saved draft notes, favorited scripts, schedule entries on your content calendar kept on your device storage via an encrypted on-device database (Android Room).
  • Device & Diagnostic Telemetry: Non-identifying technical metadata including OS version, app version code (2), network state, and crash logs to maintain stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. Generative Content Production: Synthesizing inputs into video scripts, platform-specific hooks, hashtags, and marketing copy.
  2. Personalization & Brand Kits: Storing custom brand tone, colors, and typography preferences across creation tools.
  3. Workspace Productivity: Synchronizing production due dates with Google Tasks and attaching asset briefs from Google Drive.
  4. Subscription & Quota Management: Enforcing tier limits (Free daily generations vs. Pro unlimited processing).
  5. Application Health: Diagnosing errors, resolving ANR events, and optimizing load speeds.

4. AI Processing & LLM Data Protection

Our application communicates with Google Gemini API models (including Gemini 3.5 Flash) to generate content.

No Model Training: Under our enterprise API architecture, your prompts, product URLs, video outlines, and outputs are never used to train, retrain, or improve foundational AI models. Your creative ideas remain confidential and exclusive to you.

Data sent to the Gemini API is processed transiently over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.

5. Google Workspace API User Data Policy

Service OAuth Scope Purpose & Data Handling
Google Tasks tasks Sync content schedules to Google Tasks and import task titles into prompt drafts. We do not inspect personal non-app tasks.
Google Contacts contacts.readonly Select sponsor or agency contacts to share generated script packages with one click. Contacts are never uploaded to third parties.
Google Drive Picker drive.file Select brand guides or media files from Drive for prompt context. Only user-selected files are accessed.
Google Limited Use Compliance: The app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

For users on our Free tier, the application may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-personal advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads via Android device settings.

7. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • CAMERA: Requested strictly when you capture reference photos for thumbnail styling. Never initiated in the background.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Contacting Gemini API, syncing Workspace endpoints, and loading analytics.
  • VIBRATE: Subtle haptic feedback during interactive prompt generation and copy events.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Notifying you when scheduled releases complete.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Right to Deletion: You can delete all your stored data, local drafts, and account history at any time.
  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Creator Profile > Delete Account. Purges all local databases and sign-in tokens.
  • Web Portal / Remote Request: Access our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Portal or email stephenampangwapyalenge@gmail.com with subject "Data Deletion Request - AI pro 001" to eradicate records within 48 business hours.
